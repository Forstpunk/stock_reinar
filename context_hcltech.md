INDUSTRY ECONOMICS:
IT services is a people-scaled, contract-revenue business. Revenue comes from
long-term outsourcing and project contracts. Margins depend on utilization,
wage inflation versus pricing power, and the onshore/offshore delivery mix.

COMPETITIVE POSITION:
HCLTech is one of India's largest IT services vendors, with a strategy
historically more weighted toward infrastructure management and engineering
services than pure application development, differentiating it somewhat from
TCS and Infosys's mix.

REVENUE DRIVERS:
- Large multi-year infrastructure and engineering services contracts
- Growth in cloud, AI and digital engineering project work
