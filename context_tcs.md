INDUSTRY ECONOMICS:
IT services is a people-scaled, contract-revenue business. Revenue comes from
long-term outsourcing and project contracts billed on time-and-materials or
fixed-price terms. Margins depend on utilization, wage inflation versus
pricing power, and the mix of onshore/offshore delivery. Switching costs are
moderate to high once a vendor is embedded in a client's core systems.

COMPETITIVE POSITION:
TCS is the largest Indian IT services vendor by revenue, with a diversified
client base across banking, retail and manufacturing verticals, and a
long-standing reputation for delivery reliability. It competes against
Infosys, Wipro, HCLTech and global players like Accenture on price and
capability breadth.

REVENUE DRIVERS:
- Large multi-year outsourcing contracts with Fortune 500 clients
- Growth in digital transformation and cloud migration project work
